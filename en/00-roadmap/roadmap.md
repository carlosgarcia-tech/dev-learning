# Roadmap — From 0 to Expert

Guided learning path. Order matters: each level builds on the previous one.
Check boxes as you advance.

> Levels: 1 Fundamentals · 2 Basic · 3 Intermediate · 4 Advanced · 5 Expert

- [ ] JavaScript (guides + 5 exercise levels) → level 3 at minimum

## Tips

1. **Practice more than you read**: do the exercises before peeking at the solution.
2. **Repeat by level**: if a level is hard, repeat it with another topic.
3. **Use the scripts** to create your own exercises.
4. **Document mistakes** in [10-errors](../10-errors/) whenever you hit them.
